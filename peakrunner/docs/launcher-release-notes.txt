Raindance — 0.1.0-raindance.20260919.4

Original player armor now has shaped helmets, glowing visors, articulated limbs,
boots and twin jetpack housings. Team-colored jet glow is preserved. Distant
characters use simpler geometry. Movement and hitboxes are unchanged.
Find match and secondary buttons remain readable when Windows uses light mode.

Walking keeps a steady field of view; the speed effect builds smoothly while skiing.
Flag captures play a short, team-specific musical cue.
Plasma turrets lead moving targets and check their predicted firing path.

This launcher verifies updates, downloads changed files, and supports repair and
rollback. Map files are separate from the executable. Updates pause while playing.

Compatible with the existing .20260919.3 Springdale Central server; this update
does not change the gameplay protocol or map pack.
