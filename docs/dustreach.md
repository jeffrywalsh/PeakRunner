# Dustreach

Original PeakRunner map that replaces the private Desert of Death reference
pack. It is embedded in the binary from `src/assets/maps/dustreach/` and holds
the `desert-of-death-clone` rotation slot (key kept so rotation configs keep
working). Not deployed: the live server still runs the old clone.

Built following [`map-pipeline.md`](map-pipeline.md). The user auto-approved
this replacement, so the concept's recommended answers were taken without a
separate review.

## The idea kept, and what's new

Desert of Death is a long desert crossing between two ruined strongholds, with
each flag tucked into its ruin below the surrounding ground, forward turret
ruins, and a ruined arch on the central high ground. Dustreach keeps that
idea and nothing else:

- **Kept:** long runs over open dunes; ruined sandstone strongholds with an
  exposed, sunken flag; forward and flank turret ruins; a monumental ruined
  arch on the central saddle; scattered ruin cover in the field.
- **New:** everything built. Each team's citadel stands on a raised terrace:
  a keep with twin pylon towers (spawn hall, two inventories, generator,
  baffles behind both doors), an arcaded courtyard round a sunken flag court
  open to the sky, a watch tower with the sentry turret and sensor, a broken
  watch ruin carrying the plasma turret on the right flank, and a caravan dais
  landing pad with four deploy slots on the left flank. The Sun Gate is a new
  triumphal arch whose 12 m opening the flag lane passes straight through.
  Sandstone, terracotta, bleached timber and blued iron materials, team
  banners with a sun motif, a clear desert sky and a synthesized wind loop.

## Choices

- **Name:** Dustreach (alternatives considered: Sunscar, Ochre Gate).
- **Ground bases, not floating:** the idea is ruins in the sand.
- **Flag distance 724 m:** longer than Cairnhold (532 m) to keep the
  long-crossing identity, well short of the reference's 1,150 m, which is too
  long for eight players.
- **The Sun Gate is high ground and cover only:** no neutral mechanic exists.
- **Slot:** it is intended for `desert-of-death-clone`, embedded like the other
  original maps.

## Originality

The reference was studied privately for statistics and design intent only
(notes in ignored `research/desert-study/`). No Desert of Death geometry,
heights, textures, positions or audio are copied, traced, resampled or fitted.
Terrain comes from PeakRunner's own hash noise and an authored profile;
materials, sky and wind are generated by `dustreach_materials.py` and
`build-dustreach.py`.

## Layout

- Bases at world (1024, 130, 679) facing +Z (red) and (1024, 130, 1369) facing
  −Z (blue), exactly 180° symmetric about the map centre.
- Terrain along the flag axis: a dune ridge behind each citadel, its plateau,
  a basin in front, a transverse dune crest, a dip, and the raised saddle at
  the Sun Gate (1024, 152, 1024). Diagonal crescent dunes cover the field; high
  dune banks run along both sides.
- Twelve midfield ruins (broken walls, column pairs), in 180° pairs.

| Terrain, play area | Dustreach | Reference study |
|---|---|---|
| Median slope | 16.5° | 15.4° |
| 90th percentile | 29.9° | 31.8° |
| Under 3° | 4.3% | 3.4% |
| Over 30° | 9.8% | 12.4% |

## Build and test

From `src/` with the numpy venv:

```sh
../research/local-assets/tools/venv/bin/python scripts/build-dustreach.py [OUTPUT] [--no-bake]
../research/local-assets/tools/venv/bin/python scripts/test-dustreach.py
```

The default output is ignored `local-assets/dustreach`; the build refuses to
overwrite. Baked builds are byte-identical. 2,056 collision triangles per
citadel (budget 3,000), 1,248 for the gate and ruins, 11,508 render triangles,
8 lightmap pages, pack about 12 MB.

The 18 tests cover floors and headroom, every ramp (open sky, closed
undersides), the engine's standing-support ray over every walkable region, no
accidental slopes, no overlapping floor plates, spawn validity, the sunken
flag open to the sky, transform invariance and the collision budget, unique
equipment IDs and circuits, deterministic distinct materials and sky, turret
sightlines into both keeps, terrain symmetry and slope bounds, ground under
every structure, gate symmetry and the open lane, bake determinism, the wind
loop and byte-identical rebuilds.

## Known gaps

- Distant dunes now fade into a warm haze: `sky.fogColor` 0.80 0.69 0.52.
- Kit sensor, inventory and generator models are shared; turrets now use the
  kit's mount plus the client's runtime head.
- No real glow: lit surfaces are bright textures.
- Nobody has walked, skied or played it yet.
