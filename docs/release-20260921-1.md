geometry/art and donut additions; they are not original PeakRunner assets.

Gameplay admission compares `maps2` plus all four imported pack fingerprints.
Old `.20260919.4` clients cannot join. This is fixed-collection compatibility,
not selected-map-only admission or a completed general mod/map-download system.
Raindance is external in managed installs, Skybreak stays embedded, and imported
packs live at `game/private-maps/<map-key>/`. Standalone Mac packs are in
`Contents/Resources/private-maps`; Windows/Linux packs sit beside the executable.

`ambient.f32` files; the game accepts absence only when the signed map manifest
records the empty SHA-256. Server/standalone packs preserve all seven files per
map. Fingerprints and actual audio remain identical.
